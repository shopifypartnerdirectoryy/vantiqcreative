# VANTIQ Press visual refresh and service catalog

## Goal
Bring the uploaded reference's editorial palette and service-discovery experience to VANTIQ Press while preserving the site's existing division identities.

## Changes
- Apply an ivory, deep-teal, muted-gold palette and editorial typography to the Press experience; keep Studio and Growth cinematic dark.
- Compare Press offerings against the reference and add only distinct services not already covered, avoiding promises of reviews, retailer placement, or sales.
- Replace the static Press service grid with a responsive, category-filtered card catalog. Cards will reveal useful service details and link to the existing enquiry flow with the chosen service prefilled.

## Technical details
- Reuse existing semantic CSS tokens and contact enquiry routing; do not add prices or third-party assets.
- Verify filters, card details, enquiry links, responsive rendering, and existing tests/build diagnostics.
